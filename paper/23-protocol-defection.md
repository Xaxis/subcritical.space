# Defection, coalitions and secret programmes

## Who it binds

The protocol binds the states that join and, through them, every firm in the chain. But a chokepoint's
holder can sell: the controlling state rescinded its diffusion rule four months after publishing it
[@bis2025diffusion; @bis2025rescission] and later approved H200 sales to China for a
[25%](../research/numbers.md#org-h200-fee) duty [@james2025h200]. The log makes reversal visible, not
impossible.

## The coalition share

At fixed algorithms a coalition holding a share s of the accounted fleet breaks out in h_w / s, where
h_w, the whole fleet's time to the breakout budget, is
[7.8 years](../research/numbers.md#claim-whole-fleet-horizon) on the hardwired-pause paper's inputs.
Solving that paper's threshold for the share [@fithian2026hardwired] gives s* = min(1, h_w / (m * T_r))
(Proposition 7.1): [100%](../research/numbers.md#prot-coalition-share-declared) on declared, licensed
compute at the illustrative T_r, and [58%](../research/numbers.md#prot-coalition-share-inrun-phi) once
runs absorb drift (Corollary 1.6a) and the fleet grows between renewals. The share rests on one assumed
budget, [2e+29 FLOP](../research/numbers.md#def-hwp-breakout-budget), and the overt line binds over
only [5%](../research/numbers.md#e6-budget-binding-share) of that paper's range. On paths only
historical channels see, s* is 0 until a bounded T_r is measured.

Up to h_w / T_r, self-policing needs each member's reward for reporting to exceed beta times a
breakout's worth to it (Theorem 7.2), cartel leniency's condition [@aubert2006leniency]; no fund pays a
state that, and reporters face retaliation [@hoffman-2009; @dyck2007whistle]. The penalty condition,
beta V <= (1 - beta) P, fails once a decisive advantage is worth more than
[19](../research/numbers.md#prot-prize-over-penalty) times the penalty. "Agreements have a clock"
[@gan2026clock], and against a coalition with a major power T_resp may be unbounded: deterrence only.

## Penalties and withdrawal

Without sanctions on outsiders only coalitions with minimal abatement are stable [@nordhaus2015climate;
@rahman2025nonparty]. The penalty is denial of licensed chips, cloud compute and models above the
teacher threshold, which costs a party about [2.5](../research/numbers.md#org-denial-power-multiplier)
times the power per unit of compute [@cottier2026horinger] but builds substitutes [@washenko2025euv;
@patel2025ascend].

Leaving with every path outside the line needs no notice. Otherwise, with response from notice and
assets frozen, N >= m * T_r - T_b(exiting), at most (m - 1) * T_r:
[12 months](../research/numbers.md#prot-notice-months) at the illustrative values, MIRI's figure too
[@scher2025agreement], by coincidence of our inputs. Notice alone failed under the NPT [@bunn2005npt];
licences give the freeze [@eu2005nptwithdrawal], though exit that costs too much endangers regimes
[@rosendorff2001optimal].

## Undeclared compute and secret programmes

On the published budget the smuggled stock would need
[555 years](../research/numbers.md#prot-pool-years-fixed) at fixed algorithms but
[3.9 years](../research/numbers.md#prot-pool-years-rosetta) at the Rosetta rate (our arithmetic, every
gain reaching the holder). E5's central estimate of its detection time, not a bound, is
[32 years](../research/numbers.md#e5-td-pool-years) before any response, under a transfer from past
weapons programmes that assumes a breakout as hard to see as theirs. A fast run's footprint grows with
its speed, so that figure may not hold for one; the verdict on unregistered chips survives on response
alone, since they have no licence to lapse, though in E24's record the slowest responses are not
bounded within follow-up.

So for covert paths the line reduces to "hold no covert reserve", and nothing closes the pool today.
Until it does, every party is inside the line through it, as through cloud compute and distillation,
and compliance cannot be rewarded through the line. As an interim rule, no prevention guarantee,
parties are classified on declared paths, with the pool, cloud and distillation reported as unmet
conditions common to all. The protocol shrinks the pool (registration at birth, an amnesty checked
against makers' records, then contraband status), slows the drift it can use by restricting
frontier-scale scaling research, which only buys time, and weights the channels that worked:
intelligence shared among rival blocs, and rewarded insiders. Against a large state with its own chip
chain it offers detection and deterrence, not prevention.
