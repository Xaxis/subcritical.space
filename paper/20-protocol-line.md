# The protocol: what it must do, and the line

Part II derives the protocol from Part I, each rule with its mechanism, the channel that verifies
it, that channel's blind spot and its cost to useful AI. It is a design, deployed nowhere and tested
against no real adversary.

## What the protocol must do

The protocol must keep every path of every party outside D4's line, T_b(p) >= m * (T_d(p) +
T_resp(p)), attempt by attempt; shorten detection and response; fix in advance how T_b is computed;
gate every edge between loops (Y3); and leave everything else free to improve through human-held
gates (D6). Declaring all frontier AI inside the line would meet the first four and fail the last.

## Measuring the line

T_b(p), from a party's holdings to C*, the effective compute agreed to reach D1 (D3), is tabulated
in advance by holding class under best-case assumptions, as the IAEA tabulates conversion times
[@iaea-glossary-2022]. The tables are conditional on C*, a convention, and take the fast end of an
efficiency drift E1 cannot identify; against hidden algorithms they overstate T_b. At each
declaration a path must clear the line by phi, the factor by which its T_b could fall before the
next.

Detection is computed from the channels: T_d(p, beta) = ln(1/beta) / mu at detection rate mu.
Response is not, and on E24's record no response holds a prevention line (D4). So lapse is the
default, after flexHEG [@petrie2025flexheg2; @aarne2025flexheg3]: licensors outside the holder's
bloc renew expiring licences only on logged evidence, and a failed check, a withheld signature,
silence or deadlock stops a chip within one budget of powered time. Chips lapsed on a violation are
returned, so a second attempt starts from less. The price is hold-up, and a licensor's judgement
where the evidence does not decide is not set by design.

Illustratively, the [9 months](../research/numbers.md#org-dprk-months) IAEA accountancy took to find
the DPRK's discrepancy [@iaea-gc37-1084] plus one licence period of
[3 months](../research/numbers.md#prot-licence-months) [@petrie2025flexheg2] give a T_r of
[1.0 years](../research/numbers.md#prot-tr-declared). At a margin of
[2](../research/numbers.md#prot-margin) a declared path must show
[2.0 years](../research/numbers.md#prot-line-declared), or
[4.2 years](../research/numbers.md#prot-line-declared-phi-quarter) with phi checked at each renewal.

Only that declared path can be evaluated today (D4). Cloud compute and distillation have no measured
channel, so every party able to rent compute or query a stronger model fails the line through them;
they are restricted at the source (L1, L2, L4), not lined. On undeclared paths E5's detection times
are transfers from weapons programmes that assume detection does not grow with a breakout's speed,
so a fast covert run's T_d is unknown and the verdicts on unregistered chips rest on response alone:
they hold no licence to lapse.

## The margin, and prevention near the line

The margin m, set by negotiation, budgets error as the partial-factor format of structural design
does [@jrc2008eurocodes], covering error in estimating T_b and T_r but not C*, error made on purpose
such as declaring efficiency low, phi, or the transfer of weapons-programme rates to AI.

Prevention near the line is a design choice the only calibrated model does not support: at this
work's [5%](../research/numbers.md#def-prevent-beta) tolerance it needs
[4.5](../research/numbers.md#prot-gan-prevent-over-consent) times the most monitoring Gan's parties
consent to [@gan2026clock], and such monitoring exposes secrets [@coe2020arms]. So the protocol pays
for it only where it must. A path is near the line when the interval on its T_b overlaps m * T_r, or
when it could cross within one tabulation period on the fast efficiency path; there, channels are
sized for prevention: continuous metering, chip sampling, evaluation at every checkpoint. Far from
it the regime deters.

## No loop-gain trigger, and elicited capability

With no working trigger on the loop gain (D2), the rule is the prior-work ban
[@miotti2025narrowpath; @redlines-2025]: AI-run changes to a frontier-scale system's training inputs
need logged human gates (L3), on all highly closed inputs together, since closures on separate
inputs do not multiply.

Capability is judged on D5's elicitation, under a budget named before each evaluation, at every
declared checkpoint and before deployment. Where hardened locks defeat elicitation, a system is
placed by its compute plus E1's [2.8 OOM](../research/numbers.md#e1-gpqa-overlap-oom) overlap, a
weak fallback from one benchmark that errs toward false alarms.
