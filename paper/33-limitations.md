# Limitations

The protocol is a design. Nothing in it has been deployed or tested against a real adversary: the red
team was a helper reading the work, not an opponent with resources and a motive. No AI-specific
channel has a detection rate measured against a resourced red team, so every T_d is a transfer: E5's
covert detection times, [24 years](../research/numbers.md#e5-td-weibull-years) under the Weibull fit
and [32 years](../research/numbers.md#e5-td-hist-years) at a constant rate, are central estimates
transferred from weapons programmes, not bounds.

## Where the line can be evaluated

Today the line is evaluable only on the declared path, with illustrative inputs. Every party is
inside it through the untraced pool, which nothing in the protocol closes and no experiment yet tests
closing, and through cloud compute and distillation, which have no measured channel. Useful AI stays
allowed only under the interim rule on declared paths, which is no prevention guarantee.

## What is not identified or is assumed

Public data identify neither the drift rate of algorithmic efficiency, from
[2.6](../research/numbers.md#e1-algo-annual-subset-min) to
[1,512](../research/numbers.md#e1-algo-annual-subset-max) a year across E1's samples, nor compute's
share of research input. Of in-treaty findings, [25%](../research/numbers.md#e24-halt-share-never)
were not followed by a halt within what the sources cover; the fastest halt took
[64 days](../research/numbers.md#e24-halt-min-days), the fastest exit
[31 days](../research/numbers.md#e24-exit-min-days). One OOM of C*, a convention, moves T_b by
[2.3 years](../research/numbers.md#comp-years-per-oom-ho), more than the illustrative line of
[2.0 years](../research/numbers.md#prot-line-declared).

Detection hazard is assumed independent of how fast or intensely a party breaks out, while a
breakout's footprint grows with its speed. Guarantees that a path is stopped hold as one-sided bounds
where the hazard used is a lower bound; a verdict that a path fails the line may not. The verdicts on
unregistered chips survive on response alone; the T_d figures for a fast covert run do not. The work
also assumes that effective compute is one scalar across training, inference, post-training and
distillation, so one C* serves every path, and that the parties agree on C* and the tables
[@barrett2012climate].

Not addressed: theft of weights as a pathway, a model that games the evaluations gates rest on,
collusion among log operators or licensors beyond the stated t of n, and a maker's host state
compelling the secure element's keys. Not yet run: blind grading of the pathway table, the hazard as a
function of footprint, E3, E4(b), E15 and a drill of the licensors' decision time.

## What would make the program fail

Four footings carry the protocol: a response time that can be computed, breakout times that do not
all span the line, a pool that can be closed, and host states of the chokepoints that bind
themselves, where observed compliance says little [@downs1996compliance]. The results so far bear on
the first three, and all three bear against. Against a major power with its own chip chain the
protocol offers only deterrence, of the kind Mutual Assured AI Malfunction proposes
[@hendrycks2025superintelligence].
