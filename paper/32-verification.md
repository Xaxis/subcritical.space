# The verification record

Each claim had to pass six levels or be cut or downgraded: an independent prior-art search by a helper that did not propose it (L1); a
proof, or the reason it is empirical (L2); experiments on real data or models (L3); a red team whose
only job was to break it (L4); Paperwright's integrity, reviewer and editor desks (L5), pending for
every claim; and a fresh referee who had not seen the work (L6). An application is credited prior
work used on AI; downgraded means reduced to a definition, framing, a judgement or a design. No claim
is new, and none remains a candidate.

## Each claim's standing

- **C1**: (c), a response time computed from drills, is cut because every response waited on a
  political decision; (b), the margin as an error budget, is the partial-factor format of structural
  design [@jrc2008eurocodes].
- **C2**: (a) is cut because E1 cannot identify the drift rate, (c) is untestable on public data, and
  (b) gains over one multiplier only where it decides nothing.
- **C3**: the merging rule is the textbook bound on a union, with the common-cause practice of risk
  assessment [@mosleh1998nureg5485].
- **C4**: every estimator E4 tried fails at the only calibrated noise, a human speedrun's spread, so
  the trigger is cut, untested at real round noise.
- **C5**: E3 has not run, the closest prior work is [@hofstatter2025elicitationgame], and the rates
  would cover only non-adaptive hiding on the sampled population.
- **C6**: no certificate issues while an edge is unmeasured, and E11 finds none can issue today.
- **C7**: the share is vacuous at fixed algorithms on its own inputs.
- **C8**: the floor as a bound cannot fail, the ground on which (b) was cut, the pool's multiple rests
  on an assumed pool, and E8 computes rather than tests.
- **C9**: E25, on Bitcoin's real header chain, is the strongest empirical check in the paper.
- **C10**: the endpoint cannot warn, (c) holds only while a battery's baselines last, and (b) is
  untested.
- **C11**: Theorem 11.2 is right, and the test has not run.
- **C12**: the [12](../research/numbers.md#claim-c12-pareto-two) of
  [21](../research/numbers.md#claim-c12-pairs) pairs it orders are Pareto-ordered under our grades,
  which are assumptions, and theft of weights is not ranked [@nevo2024securing].

## What level 6 changed

The referee checked the paper against its sources before reading the earlier reports. Its verdict was
revise (major), nothing fatal: no proof it followed fails, no experiment contradicts the paper's own
conclusion, and all thirteen formal checks pass on its rerun. Beyond the standings above, it raised
four points no earlier level had: E5's covert detection times are central estimates under a transfer,
not bounds; Gan's model does not support prevention at this work's tolerance; the line is evaluable
today only on the declared path; and detection is assumed independent of a breakout's intensity. None
of its findings was rejected.

## Still open

Four attacks on the claims are open: C3.8, that a state controls its insiders; C4.9, since E4(b) has
not run; C9.4, that one secure element carries every chip; and C10.4, that a system decisive in one
cluster may stay below D1. C2.8, C5.6 and C11.5 wait on E15, E3 and E4(e). Two attacks on Part II stay
open; the next section gives both as limitations.

Two published results did not reproduce, and an independent check must settle each before anything
rests on it. In E6, Proposition 2 of [@emeryxu2024uncertainty] is not a best response in our
reimplementation: a deviation gains up to [0.017](../research/numbers.md#e6-ex-prop2-br-gain-max). Of
[12](../research/numbers.md#e9-recomputed) estimates E9 recomputed on their authors' inputs,
[11](../research/numbers.md#e9-reproduced) reproduced; the growth of OpenAI's compute in an appendix
of [@ho2025experiments] did not.
