# The verification record

Each of the twelve claims must pass six levels or be cut or downgraded, never argued around. Each
verdict is recorded as the level gave it; a fix that cannot be seen in the text it names is recorded
as open. The formal checks were rerun, and all thirteen
pass. Nothing here has been tested against a real adversary.

## Levels and status words

L1 is an independent prior-art search by a helper that did not propose the claim; L2, a formal
statement and proof, or the reason the claim is empirical; L3, experiments on real data or models; L4,
a red team whose only job was to break each claim; L5, Paperwright's integrity, reviewer and editor
desks, resolved by us; L6, a reviewer who has not seen the work.

At L1 a claim is **new**, **extends** prior work (a stated, narrow difference was not found) or is
**prior work** (found in substance); "not found" is a claim about the search, not the world. At L2 it
is **proved**, **proved under assumptions**, **corrected** (false or incomplete as first stated, and
restated), **false**, **empirical** (an experiment decides) or **pending**. At L3 the results
**support** it, are **mixed** or bear **against** it, or it is **untested**. At L4 it **survives**,
**survives with corrections**, **fails**, or is **cut in part**.

A **contribution** has passed all six levels; a **candidate** has levels still to run.
**Downgraded** means reduced to a definition, framing, a judgement or a design; an **application** is
credited prior work used on AI. Each attack was **fixed**, **answered** with evidence,
**conceded** (the claim narrowed or cut), or is **open**.

Ten findings after L4 each changed a claim, Part I or Part II. A line computed at launch speed must
rise by a factor of up to [3.2](../research/numbers.md#formal-inrun-line-factor) to cover a run that
absorbs drift. The margin covers estimation error only, not phi, the fall in T_b before the next
check; with it, a declared path checked at each renewal must show
[4.2 years](../research/numbers.md#prot-line-declared-phi-quarter), not
[2.0 years](../research/numbers.md#prot-line-declared). Under repeated play with re-admission, C7(b)'s
condition (i) needs a larger reward. Compliance-only disclosure is cut, having raised disaster risk in
[100%](../research/numbers.md#e6-o2-compliance-over-none) of E6's race-model cells. E9 finds compute's
share of research input not identified. E25 finds the rate floor dominated by a powered-time budget.
E11 finds that with signed couplings rho(K) is not the runaway condition. E18 finds that a cap leaks
in jumps, so a cap's line needs the largest jump, not the mean rate. E21 finds that batteries saturate
before t* can be seen to stop. E23 finds that no version of the durability test can rank supply
stages.
