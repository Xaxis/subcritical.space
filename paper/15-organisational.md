# The organisational and economic perspective

This perspective asks who supplies the inputs to superintelligence and whether the supply can be
seen. Supply growth sets breakout time (D3); chokepoints and secret programmes set detection time.

## O1. The supply of inputs

**Definition.** For each input (capital, lithography, logic, memory, packaging, chips, power,
talent, data), O1 records the narrowest stage's concentration, growth, lead time and visibility; the
scarcest input bounds how fast a party adds compute. This is prior work [@sevilla2024scaling2030;
@khan2021supplychain; @epoch2026chipsales].

**Measurement.** Company reports, analyst trackers, vendor guidance and construction records
[@verwey2021permits].

**Evidence for and against.** The [13.6 million H100e](../research/numbers.md#org-sold-2025) of
chips sold in 2025 can be counted [@epoch2026chipsales]. Capital, talent and data are fungible,
mobile or uncountable; power, binding in the US [@rand2026queued] but not China
[@shaw2026chinasolar], is a detection channel. Concentration may be temporary
[@varadarajan2024resilience; @counterpoint2026hbm]. E22 found all
[19 observations](../research/numbers.md#e22-observations) of output inside the supply bound, which
caps allied growth between declarations at [1.65](../research/numbers.md#e22-phi-member-quarter) a
quarter but leaves room for [2.84 million H100e](../research/numbers.md#e22-china-slack) of hidden
Chinese output.

**Open questions.** The bound limits supply, not holdings; it is untested for a denied party without
a stockpile.

## O2. Race dynamics

**Definition.** O2 is the safety a developer gives up because others race, set in a model by rivals,
lead, decisiveness, enmity and time to decisive advantage. This is prior work [@armstrong2016racing;
@emeryxu2024uncertainty; @fudenberg2026cooperating; @buenodemesquita2026agirace;
@hendrycks2025superintelligence]; no model with a verification regime was found, the closest being a
bilateral pause game [@gan2026clock].

**Measurement.** Decisiveness and enmity are assumed; only the pace is seen, training compute
growing [5.3](../research/numbers.md#org-frontier-compute-growth) times a year
[@sevilla2024computegrowth].

**Evidence for and against.** Information raises risk in one model [@armstrong2016racing]; in
another public information is safer except under high decisiveness [@emeryxu2024uncertainty],
disputed by E6's unchecked reimplementation; restricting resources "may backfire"
[@buenodemesquita2026agirace]. In E6, on illustrative payoffs, declaring only that each party is
inside the line raised risk above no information in
[100%](../research/numbers.md#e6-o2-compliance-over-none) of
[72 cells](../research/numbers.md#e6-o2-cells), so that remedy is cut. Capping the prize, so that no
lead is decisive within detection and response, is unproved.

**Open questions.** C7 applies D4 to coalitions after the hardwired-pause threshold
[@fithian2026hardwired]: breakout time falls inversely with a coalition's share of the fleet, so the
response time sets the largest share, lowered by drift and by growth between declarations. It has no
number until a response is drilled; its guard against joint deviation, cartel leniency's reporting
condition [@aubert2006leniency], is empty for decisive-advantage payoffs and perhaps against a major
power. No real payoff is measured.

## O3. Chokepoints

**Definition.** A stage is a chokepoint when a few firms hold most capacity, output is counted per
unit at visible plants, and its erosion horizon (the years until an outsider replaces it at volume)
exceeds detection and response time; its leak fraction is the output escaping accounting. This is
prior work [@sastry2024compute; @shavit2023chinchilla; @scher2025agreement]. Ranking stages by
durability, and counting denial's footprint [@cottier2026horinger] as a detection gain, were not
found by our search and are not claimed.

**Measurement.** Both are modelled, on weak sources and assumed detection rates.

**Evidence for and against.** The edges leak faster than the core erodes. An estimated
[660,000 H100e](../research/numbers.md#org-smuggled-cum) was smuggled into China through 2025,
assuming [25%](../research/numbers.md#org-diversion-detect) of diversions are detected
[@juniewicz2026smuggling]; estimates of Huawei's output differ by a factor of
[4.1](../research/numbers.md#org-huawei-spread) [@zafar2025kessler; @patel2025ascend; @pan2026idc];
the United States relaxed its own rules [@bis2025rescission; @james2025h200]. Denial costs about
[2.5](../research/numbers.md#org-denial-power-multiplier) times the power per unit of compute
[@cottier2026horinger] but builds substitutes [@allen2025senate]. Ranking EUV as the most durable
stage is a judgement with hindsight: E23 ranked [9 stages](../research/numbers.md#e23-stages) on
2022 data and found r_s of [0.28](../research/numbers.md#e23-rho) (one-sided p
[0.23](../research/numbers.md#e23-p)) against later erosion.

**Open questions.** E23's power was [0.16](../research/numbers.md#e23-power-here), a decisive test
needs [85 stages](../research/numbers.md#e23-n-needed) and four years may hide slow erosion, so no
ranking is claimed. Whether denial lengthens the horizon is unsettled.

## O4. Covert supply

**Definition.** A covert programme runs outside a party's declarations; O4 records how long it went
undetected and how it was found. No novelty is claimed: the history is the table's sources, the
inspectorate's reliance on intelligence is documented [@baker2023nuclear], and the rate is our
arithmetic.

**Measurement.** A convenience sample of programmes eventually found, in weapons, not AI.

**Evidence for and against.** Intelligence, defectors, the media and disclosure found them, not
inspectors.

E5's survival fit gives [0.094/yr](../research/numbers.md#e5-hazard) from a programme's start,
overstating a careful evader's rate within the record, since all were found or disclosed; its use
for AI is an unmeasured transfer. A path only these channels see takes
[32 years](../research/numbers.md#e5-td-hist-years) to detect at a constant rate, or
[24 years](../research/numbers.md#e5-td-weibull-years) under the Weibull fit: central estimates, not
bounds, which assume detection does not grow with a breakout's speed and so do not hold for a fast
covert run. C3 takes the miss chance as a maximum over the evader's paths, merging channels with a
shared source at the larger rate, after physical-protection path analysis [@snell2007itc20], network
interdiction [@washburn1995interdiction] and common-cause practice in risk assessment
[@mosleh1998nureg5485]. Cloud and distillation have no measured channel.

**Open questions.** No channel is measured against an AI evader; withdrawal after detection is
untested.

## What this perspective gives the line

Registered compute, growing within the supply bound, sets breakout time on the declared path;
unaccounted compute should count as held by the worst-placed party, since covert work is found after
years. On the published race models the protocol should cap the prize, publish the line and weigh
disclosure against the race it may sharpen.
