# What stays allowed, and where the protocol fails

## What useful AI keeps, and at what cost

Use and serving at any scale, training below the line, agents, narrow systems, science and AI-assisted
research [@handa2025aei] stay free; only closed loops on frontier training are gated. The costs fall on
chip holders, on developers evaluated about every
[6 months](../research/numbers.md#prot-eval-interval-months) near the frontier, on open science and on
confidentiality [@coe2020arms]. At this work's [5%](../research/numbers.md#def-prevent-beta) tolerance,
not a figure of Gan's, prevention needs [4.5](../research/numbers.md#prot-gan-prevent-over-consent)
times the most monitoring his parties consent to,
[0.056/month](../research/numbers.md#prot-gan-consent-rate) [@gan2026clock]: prevention near the line
is a design choice the only calibrated model does not support.

## Where the promise is not kept

C* is fixed in effective compute while algorithms improve, so "keeps improving" holds only below a
ceiling raised only on evidence. Today the line is evaluable only on the declared path, with
illustrative inputs, and every party able to rent compute or query a stronger model fails it through
cloud and distillation, as through the untraced pool; useful AI is kept only under an interim rule that
guarantees no prevention. Precaution (the fast-path tables, the margin, phi, the in-run factor) pushes
declared holdings toward the line, and lapse by default makes every holder's compute a hostage. MIRI's
cluster cap [@scher2025agreement] costs small actors, yet a capped cluster trains a
[1e+24 FLOP](../research/numbers.md#org-miri-strict) model in
[1.0 years](../research/numbers.md#e8-cap-years-1e24-bound) at the bound.

## Where it fails

At an assumed [1,000,000 chips](../research/numbers.md#phys-chips-unaccounted), the untraced pool is,
as a point, [1,200](../research/numbers.md#e8-pool-shrink-1e26-pause) times the floor of a
[1e+26 FLOP](../research/numbers.md#phys-rahman-1e26-local) run over a pause, the floor taken as a
bound; nothing closes it. Single sites drop below the satellite line by about
[2029](../research/numbers.md#e8-cross-sat-1e26-now); above expert level nothing is guaranteed where D1
begins [@barnett2024evaluations]; weight theft is a gap. A hardware anchor broken by attack, or
compelled by its host state, fails every on-chip channel at once [@chuang2026teefail]. In E24,
[25%](../research/numbers.md#e24-halt-share-never) of in-treaty findings were not halted within what
the sources cover, and the fastest halt took [64 days](../research/numbers.md#e24-halt-min-days)
against the fastest exit's [31 days](../research/numbers.md#e24-exit-min-days) [@iaea-gc37-1084].
Detection is assumed independent of breakout speed, though faster breakouts leave larger footprints: a
guarantee that a path is stopped holds as a one-sided bound where the hazard used is a lower bound, and
a verdict that a path fails may not. Any such protocol leaves chokepoints open to their sovereigns,
building open to a state with its own chip chain, and published algorithms to all; against a major
power it offers deterrence only, as Mutual Assured AI Malfunction proposes
[@hendrycks2025superintelligence].

## What must hold

The pool must be closable by registration, amnesty, makers' records and contraband status, which
nothing tests; lapse by default must work, though only its clock has been checked (E25); C* and the
efficiency path must not leave every relevant T_b spanning the line; and the chokepoint states must
bind themselves, where treaties often ask little beyond what states would do anyway
[@downs1996compliance]. The protocol is a design, deployed nowhere and tested against no real
adversary.

## What is ours

Most of it is prior work, by our search. FlexHEG gives expiring licences, k of n renewal, lapse by
default and the powered-time counter [@petrie2025flexheg1; @petrie2025flexheg2; @aarne2025flexheg3];
MIRI's draft, thresholds, inspections and frozen withdrawal [@scher2025agreement]; others, the
IAEA-style line [@openai2023governance; @sastry2024compute], the log [@laurie2013ct;
@tomescu2017catena], the height clock [@mouallem2026alerting], inspection games and a Bitcoin beacon
[@avenhaus1996compliance; @avenhaus2002inspection; @bonneau2015bitcoinbeacon], path analysis
[@snell2007itc20; @washburn1995interdiction], the union bound with common-cause practice
[@mosleh1998nureg5485], EN 1990's partial factors for the margin [@jrc2008eurocodes], breakout from
holdings and loop bans (A Narrow Path, the hardwired-pause group, the red-lines call)
[@miotti2025narrowpath; @fithian2026hardwired; @redlines-2025], cartel leniency [@aubert2006leniency]
and Nordhaus's penalties [@nordhaus2015climate]. Never searched: the renewal evidence, the log
replicas, the mixed beacon, teacher-keyed distillation and the drift trigger for continual learning.
