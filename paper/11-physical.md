# The physical and energetic perspective

Whatever else superintelligence is, it is a computation on chips that draw power, hold state in memory and move bytes. These are its only physical inputs, the ones a verifier can check without taking a party's word. A holding is weapons-grade when its breakout time is short against the regime's time to detect and respond (D4), and breakout time is computed from held compute and algorithmic efficiency (D3). P1 is the holdings term; P2 to P4 bound how fast that compute runs and what physics forces a covert programme to leave visible. An H100-equivalent (H100e) is [990 TFLOP/s](../research/numbers.md#phys-miri-h100e) at FP16 [@scher2025agreement]; estimates assume [40%](../research/numbers.md#phys-util-ref) utilisation, inside INTELLECT-1's measured [36.2%](../research/numbers.md#phys-intellect1-mfu-low) to [41.4%](../research/numbers.md#phys-intellect1-mfu-high) [@jaghouar2024intellect1].

## P1. The compute budget

**Definition.** Training compute, C_train, is the useful operations, at a stated precision, run on every chip whose outputs determine a model's weights. Its boundary is the weights' lineage, including rollouts, distillation and synthetic data. Inference throughput is R = n * v * c for n copies at v tokens a second, with c = [2](../research/numbers.md#phys-flop-per-param-token) * N operations per token for N parameters [@pope2022]; held compute H thus supports n = H / (v * c) copies. Label: prior work. Brain-compute references [@carlsmith2020; @sandberg2008], requirements in effective compute [@davidson2023compute], held compute from public records [@epoch2026chipsales; @you2026chipowners; @epoch2025datacentershub] and compute thresholds enforced through chips [@sastry2024compute; @heim2024thresholds; @scher2025agreement] are published.

**Measurement.** Counting FP8 instead of BF16 operations multiplies an H100's peak by [2](../research/numbers.md#phys-fp8-over-bf16) [@nvidia-h100], so a cap must count chip-time at a reference precision. Utilisation, unseen from outside, varies [2.1](../research/numbers.md#phys-util-spread)-fold across measurements [@jaghouar2024intellect1; @pope2022].

**Evidence for and against.** Compute scaling is credited with [60%](../research/numbers.md#phys-compute-share-low) to [95%](../research/numbers.md#phys-compute-share-high) of language-model gains from 2012 to 2023 [@ho2024algorithmic]; on a capability index, E1 finds compute explains [18%](../research/numbers.md#e1-compute-share-subset-min) to [63%](../research/numbers.md#e1-compute-share-subset-max) of growth across samples [@epoch2026aimodels; @epoch2025eci]. Against this, compute for a fixed loss halved every [8.4 months](../research/numbers.md#phys-algo-halving) [@ho2024algorithmic], so a fixed cap leaks. Less than [100](../research/numbers.md#phys-algo-small-scale-max)-fold of the [22,000](../research/numbers.md#phys-algo-total-ho)-fold gain appears at low compute, which would make a cap leak slowly far below the frontier [@gundlach2025origin], but E1 could not confirm that direction, or identify the rate, on public data. The chips sold in 2025 could run about [140,000,000](../research/numbers.md#phys-flow-2025-copies) PaLM-sized copies at measured speed [@pope2022]. If P1 binds anything, it binds training, not running.

**Open questions.** How much compute D1 needs, and how far inference and algorithms substitute for it, are for the computational perspective. Copy counts are untested against measured throughput.

## P2. Energy per useful operation, and the headroom to physics

**Definition.** e is energy per useful operation at the facility meter: e = (chip power / chip peak) * (IT power / chip power) * (facility power / IT power) / utilisation. Its headroom has two limits: the ceiling of irreversible CMOS logic, and Landauer's bound, kT ln 2 per erased bit, which binds only irreversible operations. Label: prior work. The CMOS ceiling and its headroom are computed in [@ho2023]; Feynman's headroom to physics is quoted in [@koomey2011]; Landauer's bound [@landauer1961] bounds the brain in [@carlsmith2020]; and the same room limits hardware growth in [@davidson2025three].

**Measurement.** Colossus 1's whole chain spends [6.5](../research/numbers.md#phys-facility-over-chip) times an H100's datasheet energy per operation [@epoch-datacenters-2026; @nvidia-h100]. Power per chip differs [1.9](../research/numbers.md#phys-power-per-chip-spread)-fold between sources, so energy alone cannot certify an amount of compute.

**Evidence for and against.** Of the two limits, CMOS binds first: its ceiling, [2.9e+14 FLOP/J](../research/numbers.md#phys-cmos-ceiling-fp16) at FP16, is [205](../research/numbers.md#phys-cmos-headroom) times the H100 [@ho2023]. At trend rates [@pilz2025supercomputers; @you2025], the median headroom lasts [16 years](../research/numbers.md#phys-years-to-cmos-fast) to [18 years](../research/numbers.md#phys-years-to-cmos-slow). Reversible logic could extend it, but its reported test-chip saving and road map are vendor claims [@hulick2025; @genkina2025].

**Open questions.** Reversible logic's gains at the wall, and our measurement of the chip-side terms of e, are untested.

## P3. Memory and bandwidth

**Definition.** Memory bandwidth per copy, B_copy, bounds serial speed at small batch to v <= B_copy / (N * b) tokens a second for N parameters at b bytes each, since the weights are read once per token [@pope2022]. Interconnect bandwidth, I, bounds how far training can spread. Label: prior work: the memory wall [@gholami2024], hardware trends [@hobbhahn2023], data-movement limits [@erdil2024datamovement], low-communication training [@douillard2023diloco; @douillard2025streaming] and its governance consequences [@sevilla2025decentralized; @krys2025distributed].

**Measurement.** Both are fixed at manufacture and listed in datasheets; export rules already control memory by bandwidth density [@bis2024hbm].

**Evidence for and against.** Serial speed is capped per copy, and the cap holds in practice: PaLM took [29 ms](../research/numbers.md#phys-pope-latency) per token at small batch against a memory floor of [7.0 ms](../research/numbers.md#phys-pope-memory-bound) [@pope2022]. Interconnect no longer keeps training on one site. Streaming DiLoCo reaches [95%](../research/numbers.md#phys-streaming-util) utilisation over [1 Gbit/s](../research/numbers.md#phys-streaming-bw-low) to [5 Gbit/s](../research/numbers.md#phys-streaming-bw-high) between sites [@douillard2025streaming]. Against this, a distributed run of [1e+26 FLOP](../research/numbers.md#phys-rahman-1e26-local) keeps only [21%](../research/numbers.md#phys-rahman-1e26-eff) of its compute [@rahman2026]. The loosening matters most at and below about [1e+25 FLOP](../research/numbers.md#phys-rahman-1e25-target), where caps sit.

**Open questions.** Our tests of the bound and of the distribution tax have not run.

## P4. The footprint and its floor

**Definition.** The floor, the smallest set of traces that could produce a run of C useful operations within time T, is defined twice. Per site, it is one node under low-communication training, drawing [29 kW](../research/numbers.md#phys-node-power). In aggregate, it is N_min = C / (r * u * T) chips and E_min = C * e_min. As a bound, r is the peak at the lowest usable precision, FP8 while nothing lower trains at scale [@deepseek2024v3]; u is full utilisation; and T is the longest sensible run [@rahman2026], an assumption a racing party need not share. A channel's margin is its detection limit over the floor it sees; it can enforce a threshold only where its margin is below one. Label: extends prior work, narrowly. The aggregate floor with a time axis, the untraced pool counted against it and the need to shrink the pool are in [@fithian2026hardwired]; detection limits by facility size in [@koopmanschap2026gpu]; chip tracking and a small cluster cap in [@scher2025; @scher2025agreement]; distributed evasion in [@rahman2026]; and the resolution requirement in the IAEA's lesson on material unaccounted for [@iaea-glossary-2022]. What remains is the floor as a bound, each channel's margin and the year it crosses one, and the pool's multiple with a measured discovery rate.

**Measurement.** A satellite classifier found every facility above [10 MW](../research/numbers.md#phys-sat-reliable) and missed every one below, in a small sample [@koopmanschap2026gpu]. Thermal infrared saw no facility-scale warming over a cluster drawing at least [126 MW](../research/numbers.md#phys-jing-cluster-power) [@jing2026]. On-site metering detected [96%](../research/numbers.md#phys-meter-detect) of undisguised training runs at a [1%](../research/numbers.md#phys-meter-false-alarm) false-alarm rate, on simulated power [@kimpson2026dcpower], but cannot exclude hidden work of up to [1.16](../research/numbers.md#phys-beta-worst) times declared capacity (abstract only) [@kimpson2026b]. Chip accounting sees the aggregate, with about [1,000,000 chips](../research/numbers.md#phys-chips-unaccounted) untraced, an assumed order of magnitude [@koopmanschap2026gpu].

**Evidence for and against.** In experiment E8, the bound for a [1e+26 FLOP](../research/numbers.md#phys-rahman-1e26-local) run over the [24 months](../research/numbers.md#phys-rahman-run-pause) a pause makes rational is [800 H100e](../research/numbers.md#e8-floor-1e26-pause-bound), or [1,600 H100e](../research/numbers.md#formal-floor-bound-1e26-pause-bf16) at BF16; the formal part derives the same. The untraced pool is [340](../research/numbers.md#e8-pool-over-floor-1e26-pause-p50) times the BF16 floor ([110](../research/numbers.md#e8-pool-over-floor-1e26-pause-p05) to [1,000](../research/numbers.md#e8-pool-over-floor-1e26-pause-p95), 5th to 95th percentile) and the compute smuggled into one country [230](../research/numbers.md#e8-smuggled-over-floor-1e26-pause-p50) times; an FP8 run doubles both. Chip accounting would resolve such a run only if the pool shrank [1,200](../research/numbers.md#e8-pool-shrink-1e26-pause)-fold. E22 shows only that supply leaves room for the pool: the room it leaves for undeclared Chinese domestic output alone is [3,549](../research/numbers.md#e22-china-slack-over-floor) times the bound ([651](../research/numbers.md#e22-china-slack-central-over-floor) at central inputs). As an upper bound it can never put the pool below the floor, so it is not evidence either way. E5's historical discovery rate, an upper bound on a careful evader's, gives the pool a detection time of at least [32 years](../research/numbers.md#e5-td-pool-years). The per-site floor sits [345](../research/numbers.md#phys-sat-over-node) times below the satellite line, and even a [1e+26 FLOP](../research/numbers.md#phys-rahman-1e26-local) run on one site over [4.5 months](../research/numbers.md#phys-rahman-run-now) falls below it by [2029](../research/numbers.md#e8-cross-sat-1e26-now) ([2026](../research/numbers.md#e8-cross-sat-1e26-now-p05) to [2032](../research/numbers.md#e8-cross-sat-1e26-now-p95)). Denial helps facility channels by raising a denied party's power per unit of compute [@cottier2026horinger], but in E8 full denial delays a power channel's crossing by only [3.1 years](../research/numbers.md#e8-denial-delay-years). A proposed test, that every facility channel's margin exceeds one for a distributed run at [1e+25 FLOP](../research/numbers.md#phys-rahman-1e25-target), was cut: a one-node site sits below every facility limit whatever the run, so it cannot fail.

**Open questions.** The pool's size is assumed, and only a count, which no experiment plans, could bring it down; a distributed run's traffic and purchases are unmodelled.

## What this perspective gives the line

Of the channels modelled, only chip accounting sees the aggregate floor, so the compute half of the protocol stands on held compute counted in chip-time at a reference precision, and breakout time (D3) must count the untraced pool as a covert reserve until it shrinks below the floor. Facility, energy and heat channels corroborate for the largest programmes but cannot enforce a threshold. None of this has met a real adversary; the protocol built on it is a design.
